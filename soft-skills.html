Soft skills in practice

Distributed teamwork

Led a team of 4 on the ICMCE CNN-LSTM paper. Four authors, no shared lab: we split data prep, modelling, evaluation and writing, kept one source of truth for results, and merged the draft into a single paper we then presented.

Agile delivery: Signal

Worked in small, mentor-reviewed, task-based increments. Each revision shipped a testable slice (real-data ingestion, governance tools, RAG, agent, serverless deploy) and the report was updated with what changed and why.

Agile delivery: Sentinel

Iterated in layers: limiter, tiered blocking, metrics, agent service, then Kubernetes and a CI coverage gate, with each stage keeping the build green and the agent testable without an LLM key.

Documentation

A formal Signal report, READMEs with live-demo caveats, architecture and ethics docs, and docs that list known limitations instead of hiding them.

Communication

Explains hard ideas with everyday analogies, presented the paper at ICMCE, and writes reports so a reviewer sees what was proven and what wasn’t.

Collaboration & guidance

Works with mentor feedback at IBM, reviews and tests others’ pull requests as a Keploy fellow, and maintains Pikopod for outside contributors and users.
